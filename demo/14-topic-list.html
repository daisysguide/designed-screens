Topic List
All 7 categories. Two-phase topic model: an action-phase pill per user (Not started / Your turn / Ready to reveal / Partner's turn), and after reveal a shared alignment band (Fully · Mostly · Worth a conversation). Floating dock above bottom nav: Category / Stage grouping toggle + a "Needs you" lens chip (cyan, label only) that flattens the list to the prioritized pre-reveal queue. Collapsible groups. "Manage topics" top right. Toggle above the frame previews the "all clear" state — nothing pre-reveal is actionable, so Your-turn / Ready-to-reveal treatments and the Needs-you chip all drop away.
