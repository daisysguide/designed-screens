Open — provider mismatch on log in
The problemA returning user who signed up with Apple or Google can land here and type their email into the field instead of tapping the provider button. Nothing on the screen tells them which method they used last time.
Why it matters more nowWith phone cut, email is the only typed option, so it is the obvious thing to reach for — including for users whose account has no email/password identity at all. Apple Hide My Email makes it worse: the address on their account is a relay they have never seen and would never type.
Not caused by this changeThe mismatch exists in the current Screen 07 too. Cutting phone narrows the typed options to one, which makes it more likely to be hit, not newly possible.
Needs a decisionOptions: send the OTP and let it silently create a second account (current implied behaviour — bad); look up the address first and, on an unrecognised address, tell the user which provider that account uses; or remember the last-used method on the device and pre-emphasise it. This is ios#495 territory (two-accounts problem) and should be decided with it, not here.
Out of scope for this fileNo screen state has been drawn for it. Flagged rather than designed.
