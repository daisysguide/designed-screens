Destination, not a hub — with two exceptions. Reached via bottom nav, Home's "See full progress" pointer, or any path that returns the couple to Home Base. The only outbound actions are intentional invitations: tapping a topic inside the "Worth a conversation" revisit list jumps to that topic, and tapping a category/stage row jumps to that group's accordion on Topics. Completion, alignment, badges, and the easter-egg slot are read-only — the easter egg lives on Home, not here.
Completion
ShapeA warm headline + bar, not a stat slab. No "% done," no 3-column scorecard.
Copy"You've worked through {done} of {plan} conversations together." + "{underway} more are underway." {plan} = the user's filtered plan total (45 in this example).
Bar height10px (summary tier of the bar ladder)
Bar segmentscompleted = solid #5f45f2 · underway = translucent rgba(95,69,242,0.30) · remainder = track. No stripes — solid-translucent is the single underway treatment everywhere on the screen.
ReconciliationHeadline, bar, and per-group sums all template off the same filtered plan total.
In-between stateHeadline reframes to "Nothing revealed yet, but {N} conversations are already underway." Bar shows underway-only fill.
Alignment distribution — the heart of the screen
Eyebrow"Where you've landed so far" (static)
Note lineState-aware — see copy ladder below. Sits beneath the eyebrow, above the bar.
Bar height14px (centerpiece tier of the bar ladder)
Bar fillsSolid state colors only: green #77eaaf · lighter green #96f1c6 · orange #ffad6c. Widths proportional to counts.
RowsThree: dot · sentence-case name · Grandstander count.
Fully / MostlyIdentical plain rows. No sub-line — the old "A fine place to be." was removed (the color + framing already say "this is fine"; saying it reads defensive).
Worth row — hybridThe only special row. Fill rgba(255,173,108,0.34) + 2px #ffad6c border. Count-aware sub-line ("N conversations to come back to"). Chevron. Tap expands a revisit list.
Revisit listEach worth-a-conversation topic is its own 44px tap target with a right arrow → navigates to that topic. The deliberate "destination, not a hub" exception.
Capped peek + inline expandShow up to ~4 topics inline. When there are more, append a "Show all {N}" row that expands the rest in place — opt-in, single scroll, page just gets longer. Toggles to "Show fewer." No navigation off Progress; the overflow is fully self-contained, so it never depends on a destination that doesn't exist. The worth-row sub-line still shows the true count ("{N} conversations to come back to") — the cap limits the peek, not the number. The expand panel grows to fit (no fixed clip).
Topics dependency (future)If Topics (Screen 14) ever gains a dedicated "Worth a conversation" filter/view, the inline "Show all {N}" can become "See all {N} →" into that view. Logged as a Topics dependency, not a Progress blocker. Out of scope here.
Distribution, not scoreNever a percentage, gauge, or pass/fail. A single "% aligned" is explicitly prohibited — it would put "Worth a conversation" on the losing side of a ratio.
Zero-row behaviorCase A (some revealed, one state at 0): show the row, dimmed to ~45%. The state-aware note carries reassurance — a dimmed zero reads "not yet." Case B (nothing revealed at all): card takes its empty form — eyebrow + mechanic note + empty dashed track, no rows.
Orange-heavy holdsDesign must read calm + inviting at every proportion. The orange is a warm peach off the green→red axis; layout is a distribution (not a gauge); states are journey-ordered (green→orange, not best→worst); orange items are actionable; copy reassures at the extremes.
Copy ladder — the note line
Nothing revealed → "Once you've each answered a topic, you'll see how your answers compare."
Worth = 0 → "You're landing in the same place on just about everything."
All orange → "Early days. These are the conversations to start with."
Worth ≥ 50% → "Lots to talk through (which is the whole point, right?)."
Worth ≥ 20% → "A good mix! Plenty settled, a few worth coming back to."
Otherwise → "Mostly settled, with a few worth a closer look."

EarnedChips with deliberate emoji + quoted name.

Screen ends hereNo easter egg below badges — Progress ends after this section.
Easter egg — cut here, moved to Home
Chaos meterCut. Scoring "how different your answers have been" on a 1–10 scale pathologizes divergence, directly contradicting the orange-as-invitation framing everywhere else.
ReplacementA wry observation — the app notices something specific and true about the couple, drawn from their data (e.g. "Fastest you've ever agreed: baby names. Slowest: the budget.").
Where it livesHome (Screen 11), not Progress. Progress is already dense and the bottom is easy to miss; Home is sparse, seen every session, and already has a characterful note slot the observation can share.
Hard rules (wherever it lands)Must not score or rank divergence. Stays gated (appears only after a few reveals). Keeps the dashed AI-card treatment. Stays subordinate to Home's single next action.
On ProgressNo easter egg. The screen ends after badges.

Behavior notes
All numbers are dynamic — completion, alignment counts, breakdown rows, and the note line update as topics are completed and reveals happen.
The state-aware note picks copy from the ladder based on the current distribution; it's what makes orange-heavy holds read warm at every proportion.
Tapping the "Worth a conversation" row expands the revisit list; each item is a 44px tap target that navigates to that topic. The peek shows up to ~4 rows; "Show all {N}" expands the rest inline (no off-screen navigation), and "Show fewer" collapses back.
Breakdown rows navigate to that group's accordion on Topics. No inline accordion on Progress — Progress is reflection, Topics is exploration.
Milestone celebration animation triggers on either a full category at 100% OR a full stage at 100%. Same animation, two triggers.
Category names in Title Case verbatim. Stage names: Before Pregnancy · First Trimester · Second Trimester · Third Trimester & Early Postpartum.
Grouping toggle state persists per-user, per-screen — independent of the Topics screen's toggle.
The demo state-switcher above the phone is documentation-only — it does not appear in the shipped app.
