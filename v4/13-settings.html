Edits are non-destructive (deactivate, never delete)
Editing Stage or date updates the inputs that drive topic sort + which topics surface, and the topic list re-sorts silently underneath. The change is non-destructive: it may surface newly-relevant topics, but it never removes a started or completed topic and never deletes answers or reveals. Same deactivate-never-delete principle as partner unlink.
  • At most a quiet inline "Your topics will re-sort to match." note for Stage changes. No "Rebuilding your plan…" animation, no diff/results screen, no full-screen confirmation. Never a hard confirm step.
  • Date edits get no inline note — the re-sort still happens silently, but a date adjustment isn't a stage-level shift worth surfacing.
  • Topics that became eligible appear in the topic list; topics that became ineligible stay if they're already started or completed, and the user can still revisit them via Manage Topics.
  • Prediction Cards eligibility is recomputed (see Screen 24).
  • V1.1 may add a "Rebuilding your plan…" animation (reusing Screen 03's animation) and a diff screen showing what changed — out of scope for V1.

Partner-name fallback (app-wide principle, scoped to Screen 13)
Every string that names the partner has to render correctly when no partner is linked. Rule: use the partner's first name when linked; otherwise "your partner" / "both of you." App-wide principle; this card scopes the rule to Settings.
  • Partner activity subtext — "When Jordan answers or a reveal is ready." → "When your partner answers or a reveal is ready."
  • Partner activity off-warning — same Jordan ↔ your partner swap.
  • Founding member proud-card sub — "One purchase, lifetime access for both you and Jordan." → "One purchase, lifetime access for both of you."
  • Founding member thank-you note — "what you and Jordan tell each other" → "what you and your partner tell each other."

Strings carrying the fallback are marked with a data-partner-template attribute in the markup and re-rendered by a single renderPartnerStrings() call whenever the partner state changes (via the demo tab strip or, in production, a real state transition). The Unlink confirmation sheet's "Unlink from Jordan?" copy is exempt because it is only ever shown in the Linked state — the partner name is always known when it appears.

Pairing model — partner history is keyed to the pair
Shared reveal history is keyed to the unordered pair of account IDs, never to the connection. Unlink deactivates the pairing — it is never deleted.
  • Re-linking the same partner reactivates the pairing and restores all prior reveals (not treated as new).
  • Linking a new partner creates a fresh pairing. The user's own per-topic answers persist, so they may already sit in "waiting for partner" on topics they previously answered.
