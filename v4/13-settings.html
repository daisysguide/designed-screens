Pairing model — partner history is keyed to the pair
Shared reveal history is keyed to the unordered pair of account IDs, never to the connection. Unlink deactivates the pairing — it is never deleted.
  • Re-linking the same partner reactivates the pairing and restores all prior reveals (not treated as new).
  • Linking a new partner creates a fresh pairing. The user's own per-topic answers persist, so they may already sit in "waiting for partner" on topics they previously answered.

Key settings notes
Partner activity is now the retention-critical control — it gates the transactional pushes ("your turn", "ready to reveal") and sits at the top of Notifications, above Nudge frequency.
Nudge frequency is rescoped to engagement reminders only (stalled-topic nudges). "Don't nudge me" silences this layer alone — transactional pushes still fire if Partner activity is on.
Funny push notifications is a tone layer — it changes the voice of notifications that already fire (wry vs plain). It does not create standalone pushes.
OS-permission-denied banner uses the soft orange band, not error red — the user hasn't done anything wrong, the system state is just blocking pushes.
Guided timeline has an explainer subtext — "When on, we sequence topics based on your due date." The date row is now always shown (when Q1 applies) regardless of toggle state. The toggle controls sequencing logic only.
Name appears at the top of Account — it's the most personal and frequently referenced field. Tapping pushes a lightweight edit screen.
Phone number is read-only — identity confirmation only, no chevron, no edit flow. The label varies by auth path (Phone / Email / Connected with Apple / Connected with Google) but the row is read-only in every case.
Lifetime access sits between Phone number and Delete account. It's a navigable row whose detail screen is part receipt, part thank-you, and part home for Restore purchase + Terms + privacy.
Partner row has no avatar — avatars are being removed app-wide. Identity will ride on name + a per-partner accent color, to be confirmed at the Alignment Reveal.
Unlink deactivates the pairing rather than deleting it. Re-linking the same partner reactivates the same pairing and restores all prior reveals; linking a new partner creates a fresh pairing.
Log out uses status-error-text label (dark red on white) — destructive list rows use this same treatment for WCAG AA compliance.
Delete account is placed above Log Out — it is more permanent and warrants its own bottom sheet confirmation before anything happens.
Bottom sheets (Account Deletion, Unlink) and push sub-screens (Edit name, Partner detail, Lifetime access) are interactive in the preview — tap their entry rows to open. Swipe down, tap Cancel, or tap the overlay to dismiss sheets; "‹ Back" pops push screens.
The Q1 tab above the phone controls the Pacing date row: label, value, and visibility all change. The "Push permission" tab toggles the OS-denied banner state.
Q1 update ("I'm in a different stage now") triggers re-filter of topic plan. Topics that became eligible appear; topics that became ineligible move to the hidden state (recoverable via Manage Topics). Prediction Cards eligibility is also recomputed (see Screen 24).
