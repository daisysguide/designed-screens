Waiting for Partner
← Screen 18: Question Screen 20: Alignment Reveal →
User answered. Partner hasn't. Humor is essential — this is a potential drop-off point. The waiting feeling is carried by the brand logo breathing on the cream screen (no spinner, no lock); when the partner finishes, the mark blooms and bursts a soft gold ring before the app navigates to the reveal. An unlinked variant covers the case where there's no partner to wait on yet (user has answered but hasn't linked) — surfaces an Invite CTA instead of the Poke.

User arrives here automatically after answering the final question. They stay here until partner completes their answers — at which point the app sends a push notification and navigates them to Screen 20. "Back to home" → Screen 11. Unlinked variant: if the user has answered but has no partner linked, the screen swaps to an Invite CTA → Screen 10 (Partner Linking). The logo still breathes (calm holding state), but there's no resolve until a partner links and finishes.
Screen copy

Status sits at the top with the logo + heading + one body line; the nudge floats in the middle (Poke or Invite); the exits anchor at the bottom ("Change my answers" link above "Back to home"). "Change my answers" lives in the Exits group now — it's a backward exit, not a setting tucked under the heading. There is no left-aligned element on the screen.

Behavior notes
Three-zone layout. Phone column distributes status (top) / nudge (middle) / exits (bottom) via margin-top: auto on the nudge and exits — the page-16 .phone-screen flex override is scoped to this screen only. No other screen's phone layout is affected.
Reveal mechanic (was on-screen, now spec-only). The reveal happens the moment both partners have answered. The on-screen body line only needs to say what state the user is in ("Waiting on Jordan to finish their side."); the mechanic doesn't have to be promised in copy.
Edit window (was on-screen, now spec-only). Edits allowed until partner submits. Once partner submits, the topic transitions to "Ready to reveal" and the edit affordance is removed. If the user is mid-edit when the partner submits, the user finishes their edit; the partner's submit queues; reveal triggers only when both have a fresh submission. Unlinked variant: edits allowed anytime before revealing — no partner deadline to race.
Push notification auto-fires to partner the moment this screen loads. The Poke button is a second, manual nudge.
Poke limited to once per hour per topic to avoid spamming the partner.
Punchline on tap. The "(Pressure.)" callback lands in the .poke-confirm line after tap, not in a pre-tap explainer. Wording preserved verbatim: Sent. "No pressure. (Pressure.)"
When partner completes their answers, the app plays the bloom + ring burst, sends a push to the user, and navigates them to Screen 20 Alignment Reveal — even if they've left this screen and gone to Home.
Unlinked variant. If the user has no partner linked, "Invite your partner →" replaces the Poke nudge and routes to Screen 10 (Partner Linking). The logo still breathes (calm holding state); there's no resolve until a partner links and finishes.
Chrome exception. No ✕, no back chevron — this is a completion / hand-off, not an abandonable step. "Back to home" → Screen 11 is the single way to leave. Swipe-down also exits to Home. "Change my answers" → Screen 18 is the only backward path. No bottom nav.
Reduced motion. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, the loop and the resolve are both disabled. The logo and ring render static. The reveal still triggers — there's just no animation between waiting and navigation.
Demo state switcher above the phone (Waiting · Unlinked · Resolve ▶) is doc-page only — not part of the product UI. Resolve plays the bloom, then auto-reverts to Waiting after ~2.2s so reviewers can replay.
