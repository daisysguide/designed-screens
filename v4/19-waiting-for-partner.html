Waiting for Partner
← Screen 18: Question Screen 20: Alignment Reveal →
Three states, same daisy. Waiting (linked, partner not done yet): humor is essential — this is a potential drop-off point. The waiting feeling is carried by the brand logo breathing on the cream screen (no spinner, no lock); the Poke and exits live here. Preparing (both answered, the "for you two" synthesis is being generated): the daisy keeps breathing, copy switches to "Bringing your answers together…", and the nudge and exits disappear — it's a short, non-interactive bridge. The person who answers second lands directly here; the first answerer transitions in from waiting. A restrained wind-up (small logo pop + soft ring gather, no petals) plays and navigates to the reveal when the reveal is ready (synthesis generated or fallen back), not on partner submit — the full bloom and petal burst land on Screen 20. Unlinked: user has answered but hasn't linked — the daisy still breathes, an Invite CTA replaces the Poke, and there's no resolve until a partner links and finishes.

User arrives after answering the final question. First answerer lands in Waiting; second answerer lands directly in Preparing (no "waiting on [partner]" framing — there's no one to wait on). The first answerer transitions from Waiting into Preparing the moment the partner finishes. Preparing is a short, non-interactive bridge covering the "for you two" generation window — the daisy keeps breathing, the nudge and exits are hidden.

The resolve (wind-up + navigate to Screen 20) fires on reveal-ready, not on partner submit: the alignment state is computed instantly, but the "for you two" paragraph is model-generated and takes a few seconds. The bounded wait ends either when synthesis is ready or when the timeout falls back to the templated copy — the screen never hangs.

Topic-List entry into Preparing. A topic in the Ready to reveal state on Screen 14 routes through Preparing on the way to Screen 20 — this is the same reveal-ready resolve, just reachable from a list tap rather than from the live waiting → preparing transition. When synthesis is already cached the Preparing beat plays as the short ceremonial wind-up before the reveal lands.

19 winds up; 20 pays off. Screen 19's resolve is intentionally restrained (small logo pop, soft ring gather, no petals); Screen 20 lands the climax (full bloom + petal burst). Two clearly different magnitudes of the same daisy — see the Waiting animation spec card for the values.

"Back to home" → Screen 11 (Waiting only). WhenThe instant both partners have submitted. The screen leaves Waiting and enters Preparing.
What runsCode computes the alignment state (Fully / Mostly / Worth) — instant. Then the "for you two" synthesis kicks off — a short model call, typically a few seconds.
What the user seesThe daisy keeps breathing. Heading: "That's both of you." Body: "Bringing your answers together…" Nudge and exits are hidden. No spinner; the breathing is the loader.
Who lands hereThe second answerer enters Preparing directly (no Waiting). The first answerer transitions from Waiting → Preparing the moment the partner finishes.
ResolveWhen the synthesis lands, the wind-up plays (restrained — Screen 20 carries the climax) and the app navigates to Screen 20. If synthesis runs past the threshold, the templated fallback substitutes and the wind-up still fires — the screen never hangs.
If the user left for HomeGeneration completes server-side. A push routes them to Screen 20 with the synthesis already cached, exactly as it would have arrived if they'd stayed.
Why this existsThe reveal's emotional peak is the synthesis paragraph. Landing on Screen 20 with an empty synthesis card while it loads would deflate the moment. Preparing is the short, designed beat that hides the generation.
Preparing is not abandonable. No "Change my answers," no "Back to home," no chrome — both partners answered, so this is a few-second handoff, not a stop. Reduced-motion users still see the static state; the resolve still fires.

Behavior notes
Three-zone layout. Phone column distributes status (top) / nudge (middle) / exits (bottom) via margin-top: auto on the nudge and exits — the page-16 .phone-screen flex override is scoped to this screen only. No other screen's phone layout is affected.
Reveal mechanic (was on-screen, now spec-only). The reveal happens when both partners have answered and the "for you two" synthesis is ready (or the fallback has been substituted). The on-screen body line only needs to say what state the user is in ("Waiting on Jordan to finish their side." → "Bringing your answers together…"); the mechanic doesn't have to be promised in copy.
Lock at mutual completion. A topic locks the instant both partners have submitted. Before that, the waiting partner's answers stay editable; after that, they're not. "Change my answers" is therefore present only in Waiting; the moment the screen moves to Preparing, the control is removed (along with the rest of the exits zone).
"Change my answers" un-submits you. Entering the edit flow walks the user's submission back, so the topic is never simultaneously "you editing" and "locked." This is the mechanism that prevents editing an answer that has already been computed into the alignment.
Collisions resolve to the reveal, never to an error. If the user taps "Change my answers" at the exact instant the partner completes, the server arbitrates: if the un-submit lands before the lock, the user edits (partner keeps waiting); if the lock landed first, the change is refused and the user is routed straight to the reveal. The user never sees an "edit failed" or "locked" error state.
Editing after the lock is a different feature. Re-answering after the reveal isn't this control — it's the deferred Re-answer Flow (see CLAUDE.md repo hygiene TODOs). Unlinked variant: edits allowed anytime before linking — no partner deadline to race.
Push notification auto-fires to partner the moment this screen loads. The Poke button is a second, manual nudge.
Poke limited to once per hour per topic to avoid spamming the partner.
Punchline on tap. The "(Pressure.)" callback lands in the .poke-confirm line after tap, not in a pre-tap explainer. Wording preserved verbatim: Sent. "No pressure. (Pressure.)"
Resolve on reveal-ready, not on partner submit. When the partner completes, the screen first moves to Preparing (heading "That's both of you.", body "Bringing your answers together…") while the "for you two" synthesis generates. The wind-up (restrained logo pop + soft ring gather) + push + navigation to Screen 20 fire when synthesis is ready — or when the bounded-wait threshold expires and the templated fallback substitutes. This applies whether the user is still on the screen or has gone to Home (in which case the push routes them straight to Screen 20). The full bloom + petal burst lives on Screen 20, not here.
Second answerer entry path. The partner who finishes second is taken straight from Screen 18 to this screen in Preparing, not Waiting — there's no one to wait on. Both partners pass through the same Preparing beat before the reveal; one arrives from Waiting, one starts there.
Unlinked variant. If the user has no partner linked, "Invite your partner →" replaces the Poke nudge and routes to Screen 10 (Partner Linking). The logo still breathes (calm holding state); there's no resolve until a partner links and finishes.
Chrome exception. No ✕, no back chevron — this is a completion / hand-off, not an abandonable step. "Back to home" → Screen 11 is the single way to leave. Swipe-down also exits to Home. "Change my answers" → Screen 18 is the only backward path. No bottom nav.
Reduced motion. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, the loop and the resolve are both disabled across all three states. The logo and ring render static. Preparing still swaps to its copy and still hides the nudge + exits; the reveal-ready resolve still triggers — there's just no animation between Preparing and navigation.
Demo state switcher above the phone (Waiting · Unlinked · Preparing · Simulate ready ▶) is doc-page only — not part of the product UI. "Simulate ready" plays the wind-up from whichever of Waiting or Preparing is current, then navigates to Screen 20 — mirroring the real reveal-ready resolve (replay by returning to this screen). In the product flow the wind-up is gated on the reveal being ready (see Both answered — preparing the reveal); the demo control bypasses that gate so the resolve beat is reviewable in isolation.
